What it does

  • Answers 24/7 in your business name
  • Knows your services, service area, hours and emergency policy
  • Shares only the prices you approve, like your service-call fee
  • Emails you each job request with the transcript
  • Sends you a monthly report: calls answered, job requests, emergencies, after-hours calls

What it won't do

  • Pretend to be human. If someone asks, it says it's an AI receptionist.
  • Promise an appointment time. It takes the request; your team confirms.
  • Quote a firm price or diagnose the problem over the phone.
  • Take card numbers or payments.

Questions

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. You forward calls to your Get Jobs Booked line, usually just the ones you don't answer, so your office still picks up first.

Does it book appointments?

No. It takes the job request and tells the caller your team will call back to confirm a time. You stay in control of your schedule.

Will callers know it's AI?

It greets callers as your AI receptionist, and if anyone asks whether they're talking to a person, it says plainly that it's an AI and your team follows up.

What does it know about my business?

What you tell us at setup: services, service area, hours, emergency policy, how you handle scheduling, and any prices you want shared. If a caller asks something it doesn't know, it says your team will confirm on the callback.

How do I get the job details?

By email, right after each call, with the caller's details, a tap-to-call button and the transcript. Emergencies are flagged in the subject line. Text alerts are coming.
