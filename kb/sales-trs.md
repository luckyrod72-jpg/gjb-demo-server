TRADE READY SITES

No sales call needed. They go to tradereadysites.com/ followed by their trade (roofing, hvac, plumbing or electrical), look at the sample site and click Buy. Checkout is Stripe. After purchase they get a setup link by email. An optional 15-minute launch call is included after purchase to review the preview and collect revision notes.
