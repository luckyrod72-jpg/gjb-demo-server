GET JOBS BOOKED
What it is: a virtual receptionist for service businesses that answers every call, day or night, using the business's own information (services, service area, hours, common questions). After each call it emails the owner a job summary with the caller's details, a call-back button and a transcript. The caller hears the contractor's own business name, not Get Jobs Booked.
What it does not do: it doesn't put jobs on the owner's schedule; the owner gets the job details and calls the customer back. Texting isn't available yet.
If they ask, it's fair to say: "You're talking to the kind of receptionist your customers would hear."

Callers can phone a demo line as if they were a customer: Copperline Plumbing, a plumbing demo, at 702-602-8867.

PRICING (quote these; anything custom goes to Roderick on a callback)
- $199 a month for 350 minutes, $399 a month for 750 minutes, $699 a month for 1,400 minutes.
- Over the plan: calls are still answered; extra minutes are 60 cents each. The owner gets a heads-up at 80% of the plan.
- Billing starts on the signup day each month. Cancel anytime.
- Founding offer: the first 10 businesses pay no setup fee and get a two-week trial on their own line. The monthly price is stated before the trial, and they're billed only after they say OK. After the founding seats, there's a one-time setup fee plus the monthly plan.

COMMON QUESTIONS
- What does it know about my business? Roderick sets it up with their services, service area, hours and the questions their customers usually ask.
- Does it book appointments? It collects everything needed and emails the owner right away; the owner confirms the job.
- What if a caller wants a real person? It takes the details and the owner gets them immediately by email.
- What about my website? Mention Trade Ready Sites briefly; Roderick covers it on the callback.
